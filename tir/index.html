how to play

three beats. one race.

  1. 1

    a target word appears.

    everyone in the global room sees the same target, at the same time. you start from the same word, too.

  2. 2

    pick toward it.

    four options. the closest in meaning is the biggest leap. your current word jumps to your pick; four new options appear.

  3. 3

    first to reach wins.

    pure pairwise Elo, the same math chess uses. losing to someone far above barely costs you. beating someone far below barely earns you. honest.

semantic, not lexical. you win on what words mean, not on spelling tricks.
