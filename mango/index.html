the boundary

real now. unfinished where it matters.

real today

the launcher, unified Search, local library, household rails, phone librarian, and playback path exist in source and have been exercised on a development pi.

you bring

the Raspberry Pi, controller, accounts, addon manifests, credentials, and rights to the media you access. mango does not ship movies, iptv, or debrid.

still open

no-SSH first boot, intentional display sleep, native HDR, and whole-product couch sign-off on this public tag.

mango is an alpha self-hosted project, not a finished retail appliance. mango inserts no advertising into its own interface; third-party services remain their own. mango is not affiliated with youtube, Stremio, debrid services, IPTV providers, studios, or broadcasters.
