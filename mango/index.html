what works today

the whole viewing loop.

01

browse and search.

movies, TV shows, youtube, and optional live results share one controller-first launcher.

02

inspect before play.

open Detail, compare available quality, audio, and language, then press B. the launcher stays until mpv proves advancing media.

03

teach your taste.

Fire and Water ratings shape cached household rails. For You appears only when the evidence exists; otherwise you see labelled Top Picks.

04

ask from your phone.

the optional librarian can search and open a title on the TV. it does not speak, and it does not start playback.

the boundary

real now. unfinished where it matters.

real today

the launcher, unified Search, local library, household rails, phone librarian, and native mpv path exist in source and have been exercised on a development pi.

you bring

the Raspberry Pi, controller, accounts, addon manifests, credentials, and rights to the media you access. mango does not ship movies, iptv, or debrid.

still open

no-SSH first boot, intentional display sleep, native HDR, and whole-product couch sign-off on this public tag.

mango is an alpha self-hosted project, not a finished retail appliance. mango inserts no advertising into its own interface; third-party services remain their own. mango is not affiliated with youtube, Stremio, debrid services, IPTV providers, studios, or broadcasters.
